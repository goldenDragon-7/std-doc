2.3 §2Why raw() must die

Two reasons, and they are the same reason.
Security — the stranger's source.json
We ship a signed binary to people who write their OWN source.json. A raw() pathway lets a malicious source inject markup/script the reader's browser executes. The freeze gate stops exfil (external refs), not inline injection. Designing raw() out removes the seam entirely — the §6 'field of fear' answer made literal.
Expressiveness — a raw() is a missing primitive
Every raw() call is a place the vocabulary ran out and we punted to hand-HTML. The 40 sites are a punch-list: each becomes a structured primitive or a composition. raw()→0 is both the security gate AND the expressiveness win.
🔎

2.3 §3The residual judgment (a P3 detail, not a top crux)

One real design call, smaller than the two above.
Primitive-extraction granularity
When we dissolve statemachine, what's the right grain? Is 'swim-lane grid' one primitive or several (lane / beat / cell)? Judgment call, made during P4, provable by the golden oracle either way.

The raw() trap (what I almost blessed)

  • “Some primitives are too hard — let them inject raw HTML.”
  • A stranger's source.json can inject <script> → runs in the reader's browser.
  • Freeze gate catches external refs, NOT inline injection.
  • It's a confession the primitive vocabulary ran out.
  • Betrays covenant II (expression is the payload) and the §6 security mission.

Composition (the right answer)

  • Primitives are safe atoms: structured in, escaped out, no raw.
  • A composition is primitives nested in data — a template.
  • statemachine becomes: general primitives + a composition, raw()=0.
  • A stranger can only COMPOSE safe atoms — no injection truck-route.
  • Expression scales by growing the shared vocabulary, not by punting.
