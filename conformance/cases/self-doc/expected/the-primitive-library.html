3.5 §1All 13, rendered — the catalog IS the demo

showroom
No cryptic list. The table below is the complete catalog of all 13 first-party primitives — and it is itself rendered by the table primitive, with each name tinted by the palette engine. The chips, the band, and the two diagrams beneath it are likewise produced live by chip.py, callout.py, swimlane.py and statetrack.py as you loaded this page. (The core renders author-placed blocks after a node's sections — Move 0 ordering — so the showroom sits here, directly below this one line, not buried under a long prose list.)
Everything from here down is rendered live by the primitives it documents.
These are not screenshots or descriptions. The colored chips, this band, the 13-row table, and the swimlane + state-track diagrams below were all produced by the engine's own primitive modules, dispatched through the registry as the page loaded. A doc about a render engine, rendered by that engine.
