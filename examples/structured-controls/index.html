Structured controls — pick, don't type

A lot of feedback is really "which of these?". Let the reader answer by choosing.

Each block below is marked with data-cf-control. The feedback library turns it into a real control with a submit answer button; your pick lands in feedback/inbox.jsonl as a structured control comment the agent reads directly — no prose to parse. Add a free-text note alongside any pick if you like.

Radio — pick exactly one

The decision-matrix verdict, made answerable. One choice wins.

Checkbox — tick all that apply

Scope and blockers: any number, including none-yet.

Select — one of many, compact

When the options are self-evident or numerous, keep it to one line.

When the agent answers, it reads your value directly and locks the choice in — your pick becomes the decision, rendered right here on reload. That's the whole loop, made multiple-choice.
