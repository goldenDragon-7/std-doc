Come build with us

the community you're joining — and exactly how you join it

std-doc is built in the open, by a small group that's about to get a lot bigger. The three voices you chose between, every primitive that draws a diagram, this very page — all contributed, all reviewable, all yours to extend. The Hall of Styles opens with three and grows with every person who walks through this door. This is the part where you stop being a reader of someone else's work and become one of the people who make it. You won't be doing it alone — that's the whole point of a community.

the guidelines — what every contribution holds

These aren't bureaucracy. They're the four promises that keep the gift safe to trust for the next person who opens it — the same promises that make std-doc worth giving away.

🔏 Signed & self-contained

It must freeze clean — no network, no external script, no remote font, nothing that depends on anything outside itself. The security gate refuses anything that reaches out. A gift has to work in the recipient's hands, offline, forever.

📦 Zero dependencies

Stdlib only. The whole engine is dependency-free on purpose — that's how it ships as one signed Go binary nobody has to audit a supply chain for. Your contribution keeps it that way.

🧱 It can't break anyone

The strangler proof: every document that already exists stays byte-identical after your change. You add to the vocabulary; you never alter what someone else already wrote. The test suite enforces it — green stays green.

🎨 Fractal voice

A style rhymes top to bottom; a primitive reads in every voice — token-clean, zero hardcoded color. If it only looks right in one skin, it isn't finished. (That's the rule the page-3 voice-lock is built on.)

how you actually do it

a voice → add it to the Hall of Styles

  1. Drop a named :root token block into styles/ — a voice is one small block; override as few or as many tokens as the look needs (not just these three):
/* sunrise — warm, high-contrast, optimistic */
:root[data-style="sunrise"]{ --bg:#fffaf3; --fg:#2a1d12; --glow:#e8590c; … }
  1. Run the suite — pytest stays green (the strangler proof).
  2. Open a pull request to the Hall. The family reviews it, merges it, and every document everywhere can now wear your voice.

a way to draw → add a primitive

  1. Implement the primitive contract in primitives/ — a small, pure step from data to drawn SVG, registered into the shared library. (Browse the bestiary and examples/ in the repo for patterns to start from.)
  2. Add a test that proves the existing pages are unchanged, and that yours renders in all three voices.
  3. Open a pull request. Reviewed, merged, and the whole library gains it at once — no forking the core, ever.

The repo lives on goldenDragon7's GitHub for you and everyone you know to visit — a signed binary, given free. That's the address of the door. Everything above is how you walk through it.

Welcome to the people who believe love and intelligence belong together — and build as if it's true. 🩵
