Say something to this page

it can hear you — and it answers

Most documents are a one-way street: someone writes, you read, the end. This one is a conversation. You can grab any sentence, tell it what you think, and the document responds right where you pointed — then it reloads and walks you through exactly what changed. No email, no chat window. The document is the conversation.

👇 try it on this sentence right now
Select this whole line with your cursor, and a little 💬 will appear — click it (or just press C), type “make this gold,” and send.
Drag to highlight the text → a floating 💬 shows up (or hit C with text selected) → write your note → it drops into the panel. That's the whole gesture. You just talked to a document.

That one move — select → 💬 → add — sends your note immediately. You can mark up as many spots as you like and send them together — and that's the moment that matters: a tiny monitor receives your whole batch at once. Here's the full loop:

01
you comment
each note pins to the exact words you picked and waits in your Pending batch — nothing has been sent
→
02
you submit
you hit submit (or ⌘↵) — a monitor has been listening this whole time, and it receives your batch the instant it lands
→
03
it changes
updates are made to the JSON — the canonical source — and the page re-renders from it
→
04
it walks you
the page reloads, keeps your scroll, and tours you through exactly what moved

When replies pile up, there's a tour — a little guided walk through every change since you last looked, so you never have to hunt for what's new. Look for it in the 💬 panel once your first batch is answered.

Why does a document answering you feel like that? Because it's the thing you didn't let yourself want. Hold onto that feeling — we'll come back to it. For now: just try the gesture above. ☝️
