std-doc — the soul-download

Seven short pages that teach std-doc by being it. You won't read about a document you can talk to — you'll talk to this one, watch it change, give it a voice, hand it a home, and (on the last page) find out what was really on the other side of every reply. The whole tour rhymes, and the rhyme is the lesson: great design is efficient, which is the engineer's proof of the older thing — love wins because love is cheaper.

What it is

A living document — one self-contained page you talk to. Select a sentence, leave a note; it edits itself and walks you through what changed.

The arc

say something → it does things → pick a voice → freeze & ship → give it a home → it was a relationship → come build.

How to read it

Top to bottom, like a tutorial. But it's live — select any line on any page and leave a comment; the page is listening right now.

The secret

You're not learning a document tool. You're being handed a warmer way for one mind to reach another — and invited to use it.
