Pick your voice

Three voices, not three skins. Each tab is a consistent document top to bottom — its subject, its diagram, its buttons, the roundness of its corners. That total self-consistency is “fractal voice”, and it's the cheap path on both ends: the reader spends nothing reconciling mismatches, and the writer chooses once — after that, the voice answers every choice (which diagram, which hue, which corner), so everything flows. Choose once, generate free. And the voice goes all the way down: pick one, and it even suggests the name you'd call me.

In this voice, you can call me ’doc.just “’doc” — short for std-doc.
